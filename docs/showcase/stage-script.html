Krishna Opening 15 seconds

Stand next to Zebo, smile, wait until the room is quiet.

"Good afternoon. We're Team Nova. I'm Krishna, and with me are Zebo, Yash and Atharva.

We built ReproSense. Zebo will tell you why, and Yash and Atharva will show you, live."

Nod to Zebo and step half a pace back.

Zebo Slide 1 · The problem 25 seconds

Wait one beat. Look at the judges, not the screen. Slow down: this is a story, not a list.

"Meet Noura. She's 24, from Al Ain. (pause) She's fictional, but her story isn't.

For a year, her periods have come every six to eight weeks, with acne and extra hair. She knows something is wrong.

But in a ten-minute appointment she can't remember the dates, she feels embarrassed, and she leaves with 'come back if it gets worse'.

That's how up to seventy percent of women with PCOS end up not knowing they have it. And in a UAE study, endometriosis took eleven and a half years to diagnose.

(pause) So the problem isn't tracking. It's explaining."

Click to slide 2.

Zebo Slide 2 · The solution 30 seconds

"So ReproSense does the explaining for her.

She logs in seconds: a tap, or one sentence in Gulf Arabic.

Over the year, rules written from published guidelines spot patterns worth discussing with a doctor, and show the exact days behind each one, with the guideline. It never diagnoses. Urgent signs come first, with 998.

She can ask Nura, our assistant, what's happening in her body, and get plain words from NHS sources, with her own numbers.

And with her consent, her doctor gets a two-page report to read in a minute.

(pause) Noura doesn't have to explain any more. Her record does it for her."

Click to slide 3.

Zebo Slide 3 · Who uses it, what's next 20 seconds

"Women like Noura use it free, always. Clinics and primary-care networks subscribe, because the follow-ups that guidelines recommend actually get done, and they can see it. We'll set the price with our first pilot clinic.

Next: one clinic pilot, UAE hosting with UAE PASS sign-in, and a licensed company by May."

Click to slide 4.

Zebo Slide 4 · Hand over 5 seconds

"Here it is, live. Yash, Atharva."

Atharva switches to the browser. Zebo steps aside so the screen is clear.

Krishna Closing 10 seconds, right after the demo

"So Noura doesn't have to explain. Her record does.

We're asking for one pilot clinic, a clinical advisor to sign off our rules, and the grant. (pause) Thank you."

Then: "We'd love your questions." Take each question yourself or hand it on (see the demo script, Part C).

Zebo: if you get lost

  • Come back to one line: "The problem isn't tracking. It's explaining. ReproSense does the explaining for her."
  • Breathe before each slide. It's fine to be slower than you think.
  • A question you're unsure of: "Good question. Krishna, could you take that?"

Numbers to know (only these)

  • Up to 70% of women with PCOS don't know they have it: WHO fact sheet, 2026.
  • 11.6 years from first symptoms to an endometriosis diagnosis: 518 women in the UAE, Frontiers in Global Women's Health, 2021.
  • Safety: on 132 test messages written separately from the code and never used to tune it, all 30 crisis messages went to support and all 32 emergencies to 998.
  • Glucose test after diabetes in pregnancy: recommended 4 to 12 weeks after birth (American Diabetes Association).

Never say

  • "You have PCOS", or any diagnosis, risk or percentage for a person.
  • A price, or an AED number.
  • That a clinic or clinician has signed, or that we interviewed women (not yet).
  • "Independent reviewers" (the test messages were written separately from the code, not by outside reviewers).
  • That PC008 is a current DoH scorecard (it was retired in 2025).
