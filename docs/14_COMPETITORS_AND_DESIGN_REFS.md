# 14 · Competitors and design references

## Competitive map
| Player | Type | What they do well | Gap ReproSense fills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flo, Clue | Global cycle trackers | Polished consumer UX, huge user base | Period prediction, not clinical history; data offshore; privacy controversies (FTC action against Flo, 2021) |
| Cycla AI, OvaKind, HER EndoPCOS, Endolog, EndoTrack (2025–26) | New PCOS/endo symptom apps | Condition-specific logging | Consumer-only; no clinic workflow, no UAE context, no guideline-linked evidence |
| Nabta Health (UAE) | Hybrid women's health care | Care pathways, clinics, $4.5M raised | Provider-led service; we are software that any clinic can adopt — potential partner |
| ŌURA × DoH, Ultrahuman × ADPHC | Wearables + government research | Continuous sensor data | Signals, not a clinical next step; we can ingest wearable data as context |
| EHR modules | Hospital software | Inside the workflow | Generic, no patient-side logging or women's life-course view |

**Positioning line:** "Trackers record. ReproSense explains — and gets it to the doctor."

## Design references (study, don't copy)
| Reference | Lesson |
| --- | --- |
| Awwwards-listed health sites (Iron Health, Mave Health; community scores ~7.3/10) | Calm hero, product visible early, one strong accent, lots of whitespace |
| Apple Health / Apple Watch pages | Purposeful motion, privacy explained in plain language, big confident numbers |
| Linear, Stripe dashboards | Dense but quiet data tables, keyboard-friendly, crisp typography |
| Our own mock-ups (rose/plum glass cards) | Warmth and softness for the patient side |

## What makes the landing page "award-level"
1. A 3D glass hero that means something (life stages), not decoration.
2. The product working above the fold (live cycle ring + one observation card).
3. One scroll story: fragments → connected → doctor → clinic.
4. Perfect light/dark parity and Arabic RTL — rare and noticed by Emirati jurors.
5. Micro-interactions on evidence chips (tap → timeline jumps to that day).
