## Judge Q&A
| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is this a medical device? | It's a non-diagnostic information and consultation-prep tool with clinician in the loop; we'll confirm classification with DoH before any pilot. |
| How accurate is it? | Observations are deterministic rules from published guidelines, 100% traceable to entries. We don't claim clinical accuracy until a pilot study. |
| Why not just Flo or Clue? | They predict periods. We produce a guideline-linked, doctor-ready history and a clinic workflow — and we never monetise health data. |
| Who pays? | Women's health and IVF clinics (per clinician seat), later payers and employers for care-gap closure. |
| Data residency? | Synthetic data now; production on UAE-region cloud with a UAE-hosted model, per Federal Law No. 2 of 2019. |
| How is this different from DoH's Oura work? | Wearables give signals; we turn signals plus logs plus labs into a clinical next step. We can ingest wearable data as context. |
| Will you continue? | Yes: founders' agreement in month 1, validated MVP with a clinic in month 3, licensed company in month 6. |
