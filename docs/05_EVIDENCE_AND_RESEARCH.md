## Clinical evidence
| Fact | Source |
| --- | --- |
| PCOS affects 10–13% of women of reproductive age, and up to 70% are unaware of it | [WHO fact sheet, 22 Jan 2026](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/polycystic-ovary-syndrome) |
| Adult PCOS diagnosis needs 2 of: hyperandrogenism, irregular cycles, PCOM (ultrasound or AMH) | [MJA summary](https://www.mja.com.au/journal/2024/221/7/summary-2023-international-evidence-based-guideline-assessment-and-management) |
| Irregular cycles (>3 yrs post-menarche): < 21 or > 35 days | [Nordic perspective](https://helda.helsinki.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/ab97d92f-9773-4c9c-adc0-53178c47bd91/content) |
| 25.9% self-reported PCOS among 1,040 Emirati women aged 25–67 (UAE Healthy Future Study, 2016–2022). Self-reported, one cohort: never say "25.9% of Emirati women have PCOS" | [Int J Women's Health 2023](https://doi.org/10.2147/IJWH.S398651) |
| 1.6% of 64,722 women aged 15–45 had a PCOS ICD-10 code at one Dubai hospital (2017–2022). Different group and method from the line above, so the two are not a "gap" | [IJERPH 2023](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20095717) |
| Endometriosis diagnostic delay averages 6.8 years across 23 studies (published version of the 6.6-year preprint) | [Health Care for Women International 2025](https://doi.org/10.1080/07399332.2024.2413056) |
| UAE: 11.6 years on average from first symptoms to diagnosis among 518 women with surgically confirmed endometriosis (recruited 2018–2019) | [Frontiers in Global Women's Health 2021](https://doi.org/10.3389/fgwh.2021.708410) |
| Diagnostic delay is still present and mainly physician-driven (De Corte et al., BJOG 2025;132(2):118–130) | [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11625652/) |
| GDM: 21.3% of 2,546 pregnancies by IADPSG (27.1% by any of six criteria) in Al Ain's Mutaba'ah cohort, 2017–2021: "about 1 in 5". The 7.9–24.9% range is 2005 data quoted in that paper's introduction | [Frontiers Endocrinol 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2022.1069477) |
| After GDM, only 265 of 1,043 women (25.4%) came back for the 4–6-week glucose test at one Al Ain hospital (2013–2014). Internationally fewer than half are tested | [IJERPH 2022](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192113946), [SMFM 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajog.2022.12.315) |
| Women who had GDM are about 8 times more likely to develop type 2 diabetes (relative risk 8.3, 129 studies; Dennison et al. 2021, Diabetes Res Clin Pract 171:108625). Dubai: 26.5% of Emirati women with GDM in 2007 had type 2 diabetes by 2016 | [PubMed 33333204](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33333204/), [World J Diabetes 2021](https://doi.org/10.4239/wjd.v12.i10.1778) |
| Postpartum depression: 35% of 457 UAE mothers scored above 12 on the EPDS at least once in 6 months; at Abu Dhabi well-baby clinics 9.1% gave a non-zero answer to item 10 (so the crisis path must be calm and quick) | [Soc Psychiatry Psychiatr Epidemiol 2023](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00127-022-02372-1), research/2026-11-06/02 R25 |
| Kaggle PCOS dataset: 541 women, 10 Kerala hospitals, ~41 features | [MDPI Diagnostics 2025](https://mdpi.com/2075-4418/15/6/711) |
