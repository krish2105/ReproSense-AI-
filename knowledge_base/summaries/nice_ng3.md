## en
NICE guideline NG3 covers diabetes in pregnancy. It recommends testing for gestational diabetes with a 75 g oral glucose tolerance test at 24 to 28 weeks for women with risk factors, such as a high body mass index, a previous large baby, a family history of diabetes, or a family origin with a high rate of diabetes. Women who had gestational diabetes before are offered earlier testing.

After a pregnancy with gestational diabetes, NICE recommends a blood glucose test after birth and then a yearly check, because the risk of type 2 diabetes is higher.
