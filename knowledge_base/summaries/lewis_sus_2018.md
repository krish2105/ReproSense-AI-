## en
The System Usability Scale (SUS) is a short questionnaire of 10 statements, each answered on a five-point scale from strongly disagree to strongly agree. The answers are combined into one score from 0 to 100. The score is not a percentage. A 2018 review of its history and research describes it as widely used and recommends it when a team needs a measure of how usable people find a product.
