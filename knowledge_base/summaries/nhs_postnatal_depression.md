## en
The NHS page explains that postnatal depression is a common mental health condition that can happen after having a baby, and that it usually gets better with treatment. Symptoms include low mood, finding it hard to enjoy anything, feeling hopeless, guilty or anxious, sleep and concentration problems, and difficult feelings about the baby; they can begin in pregnancy or up to a year after the birth. Feeling down for a few days after the birth, the baby blues, is very common and usually passes within 2 weeks; if it continues or gets worse, it is important to get help. The page advises speaking to a GP, midwife or health visitor even if you have only some of the signs.
