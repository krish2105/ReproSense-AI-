## en
The Endometriosis Health Profile (EHP-30, and the shorter EHP-5) are questionnaires developed at the University of Oxford to measure how endometriosis-type symptoms affect daily life. The EHP-5 core questions cover pain, feeling in control, emotional wellbeing, social support and self-image.

They are licensed through Oxford University Innovation and are used to follow changes over time, for example before and after treatment. They do not diagnose endometriosis.
