## en
The NHS describes signs of heavy periods: needing to change a pad or tampon every one to two hours, passing blood clots larger than about 2.5 cm, bleeding through to clothes or bedding, needing two types of period product together, or bleeding for more than 7 days.

It suggests seeing a GP if heavy periods are affecting your life, if you also have pain, or if you feel very tired, which can be a sign of low iron.

If you are bleeding very heavily and feel dizzy, faint or short of breath, this needs urgent care. In the UAE call 998 or go to the nearest emergency department.
