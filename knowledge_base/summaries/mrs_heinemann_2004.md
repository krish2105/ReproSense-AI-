## en
The Menopause Rating Scale (MRS) is a short questionnaire with 11 questions about menopause-related symptoms in three groups: body symptoms such as hot flushes and joint aches, mood symptoms, and bladder and vaginal symptoms. Each question is scored from 0 to 4, giving a total from 0 to 44.

Heinemann and colleagues reviewed the scale in 2004 and found it useful for describing how strong symptoms are and how they change over time, for example before and after treatment. The score describes symptoms; it is not a diagnosis.
