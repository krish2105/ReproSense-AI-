## en
The NHS page on common breastfeeding problems lists sore or cracked nipples, a baby who finds it hard to latch, breasts that feel too full (engorgement), too much or too little milk, a blocked milk duct, infection, mastitis and tongue-tie, and says that many of these get better with help on positioning and attachment. It advises asking a midwife, health visitor or breastfeeding specialist for help early rather than waiting. A breast that is red, hot and painful can be mastitis; if it does not get better within 12 to 24 hours, or gets worse, the page advises contacting a doctor.
