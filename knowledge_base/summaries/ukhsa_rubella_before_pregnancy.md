## en
This UK public health leaflet asks women who are thinking about getting pregnant to make sure they are protected against German measles (rubella), because catching it in pregnancy can harm the baby. It advises having the measles, mumps and rubella (MMR) vaccine about one month before trying to conceive. Women who are unsure whether they are protected can check their vaccination record or ask their doctor.
