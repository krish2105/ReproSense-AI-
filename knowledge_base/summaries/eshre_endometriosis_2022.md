## en
The European Society of Human Reproduction and Embryology (ESHRE) published its endometriosis guideline in 2022. It lists symptoms that should make a doctor think about endometriosis: painful periods, deep pain during or after sex, pain with bowel movements or passing urine around periods, tiredness, and difficulty getting pregnant.

The guideline says a doctor can consider endometriosis from symptoms, examination and imaging such as ultrasound or MRI. Keyhole surgery (laparoscopy) is no longer required before treatment can begin.

Recording when pain happens, how strong it is and how it affects daily life helps the conversation with a gynaecologist.
