## en
In July 2025 the Department of Health – Abu Dhabi launched the Integrated Postnatal Care Programme under its Women's and Children's Health Strategy. It combines telemedicine in the first days after leaving hospital, visits to a hospital or primary care clinic at 7 to 14 days and again at 6 weeks after birth, and two home visits by a nurse or midwife for mothers who had complications or whose babies need extra care.
