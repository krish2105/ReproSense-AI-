## en
NICE fertility guidance (NG257, 2026, replacing CG156) covers when and how fertility problems are assessed. People who have been trying to conceive for a year without success should be offered an assessment, and both partners can be assessed together.

Earlier assessment is suggested for women aged 36 or over, and for anyone with a known reason for concern, such as irregular or absent periods, known endometriosis or a past pelvic infection.

The guidance also covers healthy steps before pregnancy, such as taking folic acid.
