## en
The Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS) was published by Cox, Holden and Sagovsky in 1987. It has 10 short questions about the past seven days. Each answer scores 0 to 3, so the total runs from 0 to 30.

A total of 10 or more is commonly used as a sign that a conversation with a doctor or midwife would be worthwhile. Question 10 asks about thoughts of self-harm; any answer other than "never" needs prompt support, whatever the total.

The EPDS is a screening questionnaire. A score is not a diagnosis, and a health professional should talk it through with you.
