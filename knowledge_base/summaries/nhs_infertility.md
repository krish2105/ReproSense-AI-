## en
The NHS page explains that more than 8 out of 10 couples where the woman is under 40 will conceive within a year of regular unprotected sex. It suggests seeing a GP after a year of trying, or sooner for women aged 36 and over or anyone who already knows they may have fertility problems. A GP can check for common causes of fertility problems, and treatments include medical treatment for lack of regular ovulation, surgical procedures and assisted conception.
