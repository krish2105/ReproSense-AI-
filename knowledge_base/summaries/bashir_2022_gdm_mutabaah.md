## en
The Mutaba'ah Study follows mothers and their children in Al Ain. Across 2,546 pregnancies, 21.3% met the IADPSG criteria for diabetes in pregnancy (gestational diabetes). The study compared six sets of diagnostic criteria, and the share depends on which set is used, so any comparison has to use the same criteria.
