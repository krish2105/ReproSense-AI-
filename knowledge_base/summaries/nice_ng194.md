## en
NICE guideline NG194 covers care for women and babies in the first weeks after birth. It lists symptoms that need urgent help: sudden or very heavy vaginal bleeding, a fever, a severe headache with changes in vision, chest pain or breathlessness, and pain or swelling in one leg. In the UAE call 998.

NICE also asks health professionals to check the mother's emotional wellbeing at every postnatal contact.
