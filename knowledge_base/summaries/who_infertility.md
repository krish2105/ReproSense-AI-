## en
The World Health Organization describes infertility as not becoming pregnant after 12 months or more of trying. WHO estimates that around one in six people of reproductive age is affected at some point in their life.

Fertility problems can come from either partner, or both, and sometimes no cause is found. WHO stresses that people should have access to fair, affordable fertility care and clear information.
