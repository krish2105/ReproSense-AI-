## en
The American Diabetes Association Standards of Care recommend testing for gestational diabetes at 24 to 28 weeks of pregnancy in people not already known to have diabetes, usually with an oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT).

After gestational diabetes, the risk of type 2 diabetes later in life is higher. The Standards recommend a 75 g oral glucose tolerance test 4 to 12 weeks after birth, using the usual non-pregnancy criteria.

After that, regular checks for diabetes or prediabetes are recommended for life, at least every one to three years. Planning another pregnancy is a good time to ask for a check.
