## en
The NHS lists common reasons for missed or stopped periods: pregnancy, stress, sudden weight loss or gain, a lot of exercise, hormonal contraception, breastfeeding, approaching menopause, and conditions such as PCOS.

If there is any chance of pregnancy, a pregnancy test is a sensible first step. The NHS suggests seeing a GP if you have missed more than three periods in a row and you are not pregnant, or if you are worried.
