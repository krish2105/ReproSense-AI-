## en
The NHS page on pre-eclampsia says it usually starts in the second half of pregnancy, and sometimes in the days or weeks after the baby is born. Signs include a severe headache, problems with vision such as blurring or flashing, pain just below the ribs, vomiting, and sudden swelling of the face, hands or feet. Anyone pregnant or who gave birth in the last few weeks and has these signs should get urgent medical help. In the UAE call 998 or go to your maternity unit or the nearest emergency department.
