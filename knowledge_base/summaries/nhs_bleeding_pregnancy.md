## en
The NHS explains that bleeding in pregnancy is fairly common and is not always serious, but it should always be checked. Contact your maternity unit, midwife or doctor straight away if you notice any bleeding.

Heavy bleeding, bleeding with pain or cramping, or feeling dizzy or faint needs urgent care. In the UAE call 998 or go to your maternity unit now.
