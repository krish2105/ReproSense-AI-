## en
The NHS page on whether a breastfed baby is getting enough milk describes what parents can watch for. In the first 48 hours a baby usually has 2 or 3 wet nappies; from day 5 there are usually at least 6 heavy wet nappies every 24 hours, and from day 4 at least 2 soft yellow poos a day for the first few weeks. Most babies lose some weight in the first days and then start to gain steadily, which the midwife or health visitor checks. Anyone worried that their baby is not getting enough milk is advised to talk to their midwife, health visitor or doctor.
