## en
A study at one hospital in Al Ain looked at 1,043 women who had diabetes in pregnancy (gestational diabetes) and gave birth in 2013 and 2014. Only 265 of them (25.4%) came back for the recommended glucose tolerance test about 4 to 6 weeks after the birth. Almost none were tested later. The data are from one hospital and are now more than ten years old, so they are a reference point, not a current rate for any clinic.
