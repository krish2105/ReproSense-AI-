## en
The Department of Health – Abu Dhabi's Standard for Well-Child Visits (revised, published December 2025) sets the routine check-ups for children from 1 month to 6 years at licensed clinics in Abu Dhabi. In the first year the visits fall at 1, 2, 4, 6 and 9 months, and the next one at 12 months. At each visit the doctor or nurse measures weight, length and head size on the WHO growth charts, examines the baby, checks the eyes and hips, asks about feeding, sleep and development, and talks about the vaccinations and vitamin D for the baby's age.

A baby whose hearing was not screened at birth has it done at the 1- or 2-month visit. The visits also cover breastfeeding, safe sleep and keeping the baby safe at home. For UAE nationals they are covered by Thiqa as preventive care; other insurance plans cover them according to the policy.
