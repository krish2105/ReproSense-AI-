## en
In September 2026 the Department of Health – Abu Dhabi announced the Abu Dhabi Programme for the Future of Reproductive Health, with a roadmap to 2030. It focuses on prevention, preconception health, early screening, fertility diagnosis, and the use of data and artificial intelligence to support care.
