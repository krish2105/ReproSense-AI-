## en
The Department of Health – Abu Dhabi asks primary-care providers to report quality indicators every quarter under its Jawda programme. Indicator PC008, "Post-Partum Follow-Up and Care Coordination", looks at women seen 7 to 84 days after giving birth. It counts how many of them had breastfeeding support, a check for postnatal depression and, if they had diabetes in pregnancy, a glucose test. The indicator applies to family-medicine and GP visits and is measured from claims and medical records.
