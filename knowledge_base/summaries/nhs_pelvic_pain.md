## en
The NHS says pelvic pain has many causes, including period pain and pain around ovulation. Sudden, severe pelvic pain needs urgent care, especially with a high temperature, vomiting, fainting, or if you could be pregnant. In the UAE call 998 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Pelvic pain that keeps coming back or lasts a long time should be checked by a GP or gynaecologist. Noting when it happens and what makes it better or worse helps the doctor.
