## en
The American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG) notes that period pain is very common, especially in younger people. Pain that keeps someone from school, work or usual activities, or that does not improve with first treatments such as anti-inflammatory pain relief or hormonal options, should be looked at more closely.

ACOG advises that ongoing severe period pain can be a sign of endometriosis and that it deserves an evaluation rather than being dismissed as normal.
