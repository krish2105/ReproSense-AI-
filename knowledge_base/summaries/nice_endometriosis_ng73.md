## en
NICE guideline NG73 covers how endometriosis is recognised and managed. It asks doctors to consider endometriosis when someone has one or more of: long-lasting pelvic pain, period pain that affects daily activities and quality of life, deep pain during or after sex, painful bowel movements around periods, or pain passing urine around periods.

NICE notes that endometriosis can be hard to recognise and that a symptom diary helps. If symptoms are severe, keep coming back, or do not improve with first treatments, a referral to a gynaecology service can be considered.
