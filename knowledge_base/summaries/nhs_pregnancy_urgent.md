## en
The NHS lists symptoms in pregnancy that should never be ignored: vaginal bleeding, a change in the baby's usual pattern of movements, a severe headache, problems with vision, sudden swelling of the face, hands or feet, severe tummy pain, a high temperature, and fluid leaking from the vagina.

If any of these happen, contact your maternity unit straight away. In an emergency in the UAE call 998.
