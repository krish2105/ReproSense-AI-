## en
The NHS page on polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS, which the NHS now calls PMOS) lists common signs: irregular periods or long gaps between periods, difficulty getting pregnant, excess hair growth or hair loss, oily skin or acne, and weight gain or difficulty losing weight.

It advises seeing a GP if you think you might have it or if the symptoms affect everyday life. A doctor may suggest blood tests to check hormone levels and an ultrasound scan.

Symptoms can be managed with lifestyle changes and treatments, and a doctor can explain the options that suit you.
