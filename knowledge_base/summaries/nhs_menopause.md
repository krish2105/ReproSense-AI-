## en
The NHS page explains that perimenopause is when periods become irregular and menopause is when they stop altogether; menopause is defined as no period for 12 months when not using hormonal contraception. It is caused by a fall in hormone levels and usually happens between 45 and 55, but it can happen earlier. Common symptoms include hot flushes, low mood, sleep problems and memory problems. There is no routine test, and a doctor may diagnose it from age and symptoms. Menopause before 45 is called early menopause.
