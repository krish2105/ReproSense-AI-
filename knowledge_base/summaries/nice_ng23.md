## en
NICE guideline NG23 covers menopause and perimenopause. For otherwise healthy women over 45, NICE says perimenopause can usually be recognised from symptoms such as hot flushes and night sweats together with changing periods, without blood tests. Menopause is usually recognised after 12 months without a period in women not using hormonal contraception.

Effective treatments exist, including hormone replacement therapy and, for some symptoms, cognitive behavioural therapy. A doctor can talk through the benefits and risks for each person.

Any bleeding after periods have stopped for 12 months should be checked by a doctor.
