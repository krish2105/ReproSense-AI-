## en
The NHS advises taking 400 micrograms of folic acid every day while trying to get pregnant and until 12 weeks of pregnancy. Folic acid helps prevent problems in the baby's spine and brain development.

Some people are advised to take a higher dose, for example with diabetes or certain medicines; a doctor prescribes this. The NHS also recommends 10 micrograms of vitamin D a day during pregnancy and breastfeeding.
