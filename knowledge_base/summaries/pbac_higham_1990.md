## en
The pictorial blood loss assessment chart (PBAC) was described by Higham and colleagues in 1990. For each day of a period you count pads and tampons by how stained they are, and the clots you pass.

Scoring: a lightly stained pad counts 1, a moderately stained pad 5 and a fully soaked pad 20. For tampons the scores are 1, 5 and 10. A small clot counts 1 and a large clot 5. The scores are added up for the whole period.

In the original study, a total of 100 or more matched a measured blood loss of more than 80 mL, which is the usual research definition of heavy menstrual bleeding. The chart is a guide to share with your doctor, not a diagnosis.
