## en
NICE guideline CG192 covers mental health during pregnancy and in the year after birth. Low mood and anxiety after birth are common and treatable. NICE asks health professionals to ask about mood at the first contact and at later appointments, and to consider using a short questionnaire such as the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale as part of a full assessment.

Anyone who has thoughts of harming themselves needs support straight away. In the UAE call 998 in an emergency, or speak to a doctor today.
