## en
NICE guideline NG201 covers routine care in pregnancy: the timing of appointments, the tests offered, and the information pregnant women should receive.

It asks that women are told which symptoms need urgent advice, including vaginal bleeding, severe abdominal pain, a change or reduction in the baby's movements, and a severe headache with problems with vision, which can be signs of pre-eclampsia. In the UAE call 998 or go to your maternity unit if these happen.
