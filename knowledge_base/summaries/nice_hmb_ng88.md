## en
NICE guideline NG88 describes heavy menstrual bleeding as blood loss during periods that interferes with physical, emotional, social or practical quality of life. NICE focuses on how bleeding affects a person's life rather than on measuring the exact amount.

NICE advises a full blood count for anyone with heavy menstrual bleeding, because heavy periods are a common cause of low iron and anaemia. It says serum ferritin should not be tested routinely, only when a doctor thinks it is needed.

Treatment options range from medicines to hormonal devices and procedures, and are chosen together with the doctor.
