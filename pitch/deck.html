The demo, in three minutes.

  1. Noura's patterns each show the days and the guideline behind them.
  2. Asked in Arabic, the assistant answers with its source.
  3. A two-page PDF, shared through a link that expires.
  4. The clinician view is separate: handover, evidence, visit-note draft.
  5. Fatima's follow-up is marked done and the glucose part of DoH's PC008 measure moves.

PC008: DoH Jawda primary-care quality indicators, version 7. Counts are from the synthetic cohort plus the live demo.
