The history exists. It arrives at the appointment in pieces.

In the UAE Healthy Future Study, a quarter of Emirati women reported PCOS. In a large Dubai hospital record review, it was coded for fewer than 2 in 100 women. The gap is recognition, not prevalence.

Endometriosis takes years to name. Across 22 studies the average was 6.6 years from first symptoms, and women often describe not being believed.

Gestational diabetes is common in the UAE. After the birth, the glucose test that catches type 2 diabetes early is often skipped, because it falls between the maternity team and primary care.

Abu Dhabi is already moving: the Department of Health launched its Programme for the Future of Reproductive Health in September 2026, built on prevention and early screening.

Follow one observation from her phone to the clinic.

Rules decide, published guidelines ground, and the language model only explains. This is Noura's path through the system, using her synthetic data.

Two people use it. One pays.

She keeps the record

Logging takes about twenty seconds: flow, pain from 0 to 10, a bleeding score using the same pictorial chart gynaecologists use, or a sentence in Arabic or English. She sees her year on one screen, gets questions to ask, and decides who sees her report and for how long.

Free for women. No advertising. Health data is never sold.

The clinic acts on it

Before the visit the clinician receives a structured history in a standard handover format, the evidence behind each pattern, and extracted lab values. The clinic dashboard lists follow-ups that are due, such as glucose tests after gestational diabetes, and shows them closing.

Subscription per clinician seat (price to validate in clinic interviews).

What we promise about her data.

No name needed
She signs in with a passkey (Face ID or fingerprint) under an alias.
She controls sharing
Each share is for one clinic, expires after 7 days and can be stopped at any time.
Every view is logged
A tamper-evident audit log powers a "who saw my record" timeline in her app.
Stays in the UAE
Production runs in a UAE cloud region with a UAE-hosted model, in line with federal health-data law and Abu Dhabi's ADHICS standard.
AI never decides
Observations come from written rules with sources. The model writes explanations and is blocked from diagnostic language.
